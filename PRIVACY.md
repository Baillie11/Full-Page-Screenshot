# Full Page Screenshot privacy policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Full Page Screenshot is a Chrome extension provided by Click eCommerce.

## Information processed

When you use the extension, it reads the selected tab's URL to display it in the popup and processes that webpage's visible and loaded content to create a PNG screenshot. A screenshot may contain personal or sensitive information displayed on the page you choose. Capture only information you intend to keep or share.

All capture processing takes place locally in your browser. The extension does not transmit page content, URLs or screenshots to Click eCommerce or any third party. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, telemetry or remote capture server.

## Storage and sharing

Screenshot data is held temporarily while capturing and copying. The extension writes the image to your clipboard and, if you enable Save to file, saves a PNG in your configured Downloads location. It does not read your clipboard. It does not maintain a separate browsing history or screenshot database.

You control downloaded files and can delete them using your operating system. Clipboard contents may persist until replaced or cleared. Any clipboard synchronisation, backup or subsequent sharing you enable is controlled by your operating system or other applications, not by this extension.

## Permissions

- **activeTab:** access the selected tab after your toolbar action.
- **debugger:** temporarily use Chrome's capture functions to obtain the complete page image; no network-traffic monitoring.
- **scripting:** temporarily expand and restore scrollable page containers when you select that option.
- **downloads:** save the requested PNG when file saving is selected.
- **clipboardWrite:** copy the captured PNG to the clipboard; never read clipboard contents.

## Limited use

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information is used only to provide the user-requested screenshot feature. It is not sold, used for advertising, transferred for unrelated purposes, or used to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility.
